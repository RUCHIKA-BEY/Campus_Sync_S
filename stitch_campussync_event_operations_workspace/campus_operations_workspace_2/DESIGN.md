## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a focused, dependable institutional workspace engineered for student leaders, administrative officers, and facility coordinators. Rejecting both sterile academic ERPs and frivolous social novelties, the product embraces a calm, utility-first clarity inspired by high-output productivity tools.

- **Personality & Tone:** Methodical, responsive, and approachable. It reduces cognitive friction during time-sensitive scheduling, venue approvals, and On-Duty workflows.
- **Design Style:** Modern Corporate & Systematic Utility. The interface prioritizes clean white and off-white surfaces, hairline borders, consistent typographic cadence, and restrained semantic cues rather than loud decorative gradients or disruptive glassy layers.
- **Visual Distinction:** Graphic event posters and documentary campus photography provide organic vibrancy, while the surrounding functional frame remains disciplined, clear, and predictable.

## Colors

The color palette centers on functional restraint to ensure long-term ergonomic comfort across both dense desktop dashboards and quick mobile touchpoints.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#F8F9FA`):** An airy, warm off-white canvas that isolates functional cards and limits display glare.
- **Surface Cards (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white for cards, timetable blocks, modals, and sliding review sheets.
- **Hairline Borders (`#E5E7EB` / `#D1D5DB`):** Soft outlines separating functional groups without creating ocular gridlock.

### Typography & Content
- **Text Primary (`#111827`):** High-contrast deep ink for labels, header hierarchies, and essential tabular records.
- **Text Secondary (`#4B5563`):** Neutral slate for auxiliary details, column descriptions, and subheadings.
- **Text Muted (`#9CA3AF`):** Soft stone reserved for placeholder inputs, inactive icons, and auxiliary breadcrumb dividers.

### Semantics & Accents
- **Primary Interactive (`#4F46E5`):** Reserved strictly for actionable highlights: primary buttons, active navigation markers, selection outlines, and verified badges.
- **Warm Apricot (`#F59E0B` / `#FEF3C7`):** Used deliberately for club identity badges, non-critical highlights, and event category tags.
- **Status Signals:** Emerald Green (`#10B981` / `#ECFDF5`) indicates approved bookings, clearance, and on-schedule events; Amber (`#F59E0B` / `#FFFBEB`) handles pending reviews, warnings, or tentative holds; Rose (`#EF4444` / `#FEF2F2`) signals timetable conflicts, denied permits, or cancellations.

## Typography

The design system relies entirely on Inter across all touchpoints, emphasizing clean geometric metrics, open apertures, and rigorous alignment.

- **Tabular Numerals:** For timetables, student counts, room capacities, and QR check-in ratios, enable `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to maintain vertical alignment in dense tables.
- **Hierarchy & Scale:** Headings stay modest in size to preserve vertical screen density for critical review items. Headlines set context cleanly without forcing actionable tables and cards below the viewport.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Micro-labels in status badges, navigation bars, and table column titles employ medium weight (`500`) with subtle positive letter-spacing to safeguard legibility at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an 8px grid (with a 4px sub-grid for internal component spacing) designed to adapt seamlessly across multi-role workflows.

### Device-Specific Layout Models
- **Student (Mobile-First):** Single-column stacked stream with fixed bottom navigation (Home, Discover, My Events, Timetable, Profile). Tap targets maintain a minimum 44px height. Horizontal carousels handle quick event discovery chips.
- **Organizer & Admin (Desktop-First):** Persistent 240px collateral navigation sidebar, fixed breadcrumb top-bar, and a fluid multi-column workbench utilizing a 12-column layout with 24px gutters.
- **DSA Approvals (Split Workspace):** 50/50 dual-pane layout when resolving On-Duty requests or venue conflicts: proposal event metadata stays pinned on the left, while approval checklists, timetable overlays, and audit trails occupy the right.

### Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 16px outer margins, stacked card layouts, bottom sheets for filters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 24px outer margins, 2-column event grids, drawer-based secondary inspectors.
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 32px outer margins, max-width constraints on wide forms (1280px), multi-column operational tables.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp boundary lines and subtle, low-opacity ambient shadows to retain clarity without distraction.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Base page canvas (`#F8F9FA`) with zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** Default state for white workspace cards, schedule tiles, and data rows. Bounded by `1px solid #E5E7EB` with an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating):** Quick-filter dropdowns, autocomplete menus, and active hover cards. Surrounded by `1px solid #D1D5DB` with a soft lift: `0 4px 12px 0 rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Slide-Overs):** Modals, QR scanners, and DSA inspection slide-over drawers. Supported by a neutral backdrop scrim (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.4)`) and elevated with `0 16px 32px -4px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The interface adopts a disciplined roundedness scale (`roundedness: 2`) that brings approachable modern structure without softening into excessive playfulness.

- **Corner Radius Scale:**
  - Base components (inputs, buttons, list cells, chips): `0.5rem` (8px).
  - Intermediate cards, calendar intervals, and modal sheets: `1rem` (16px).
  - Large structural panels and review sheets: `1.5rem` (24px).
  - Micro status pills, badge indicators, and avatars: `9999px` (Full circle/pill).
- **Poster Constraints:** Event preview thumbnails adhere to strict geometric aspects: `16:9` or `3:2` for horizontal listing banners and `1:1` for detail badges, clipped with `0.5rem` corners to eliminate visual fraying.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#4F46E5`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, border none, border-radius `0.5rem`. Subtle hover to `#4338CA`. Active state scales smoothly to `0.98`.
- **Secondary:** Background `#FFFFFF`, foreground `#111827`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`. Hover shifts to `#F9FAFB` with border `#D1D5DB`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#FEF2F2`, foreground `#EF4444`, border `1px solid #FEE2E2`. Hover shifts to `#FEE2E2`.
- **Heights:** 40px for mobile touch targets and primary actions; 32px for compact table rows and action bars.

### Chips & Badges
- **Club Identity Chips:** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`, border-radius `9999px`, padding `4px 10px`.
- **Status Badges:** Compact pills with an internal 6px status dot.
  - *Approved / No Conflict:* Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, dot `#10B981`.
  - *Pending Attention:* Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, dot `#F59E0B`.
  - *Conflict / Rejected:* Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, dot `#EF4444`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 40px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D1D5DB`, border-radius `0.5rem`, padding `0 12px`, text `#111827`. Focus state applies border `#4F46E5` with an iris halo `0 0 0 3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radio Toggles:** 18x18px, border `1.5px solid #D1D5DB`, radius `4px` (checkbox) or `50%` (radio). Checked state fills `#4F46E5` with white iconography.

### Cards & Timetable Tiles
- **Event Cards:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, radius `1rem`, padding `1rem`. Incorporates poster art at standard aspect ratios with typographic fallbacks featuring club abbreviation monograms over neutral stone backdrops.
- **Timetable Slot:** Height 56px, left accent line (3px solid `#4F46E5` for registered, `#10B981` for approved OD), background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, radius `0.5rem`.

### QR Check-in & Review Drawers
- **QR Viewfinder Container:** Centralized 240x240px card with high-contrast framing, rounded `1rem`, with real-time green/red pulse confirmation banners.
- **DSA Review Split Sheet:** Fixed right-side panel on desktop (`min-width: 440px`), clean vertical sections separated by `#E5E7EB` borders, quick approve/reject floating actions fixed at the base.
